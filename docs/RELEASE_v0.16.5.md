# PAGASA SYNOP Validator v0.16.5

Release date: 13 October 2026. Released by the site maintainer after alarm review; this label does not represent agency certification.

## Use the observation alarm

1. Open **Alarm OFF - Settings**.
2. Enter your station number, minutes after the observation (default 8; 1-120), and schedule (all 3-hourly or main only).
3. Choose Urgent beeps, Ringing alarm or Chime. Alternatively upload an MP3/WAV up to 1 MB.
4. Use **Preview sound** and **Stop preview**. Check device volume.
5. Select **Alarm ON**, then **Save settings**.
6. Keep the page open and computer awake. At the deadline, a missing Validate entry triggers the reminder. Validate the matching observation, Dismiss, Snooze five minutes, or switch OFF.

A Validate entry counts even if errors are found. The alarm does not verify transmission or correction completion. Typing alone does not count. Match UTC date and station. First activation and changes to station/schedule/delay start with the next deadline. Each browser/site stores its own settings, sounds and entries. A reload may require Preview sound to enable audio. Background tabs and sleeping devices may delay or prevent reminders.
