# PAGASA SYNOP Validator Update - v0.16.5

The updated main validator is now available!

New: an optional observation alarm with adjustable minutes after observation time and a choice of all three-hourly or main-only reports. Choose urgent beeps, ringing alarm, chime or your own MP3/WAV. Preview the sound and save settings. Dismiss and Snooze are available.

Also included from v0.16.4:
- Error and warning highlights inside the code, plus Copy code.
- Three-hour intermediate and six-hour main rainfall input, matching-period auto-selection, and T/Trace support.
- Saved rainfall review popup and 24-hour rainfall checking.
- Warning when an exact six-hour amount is below a saved three-hour amount within that period.
- Tayabas and Tanay station-pressure history comparisons.

Alarm is OFF by default. Keep the page open and computer awake when using it; a Validate entry acknowledges the matching observation even if findings remain.
